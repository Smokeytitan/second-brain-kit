# Launch Tiers

**Status:** Active v1.0
**Owner:** {PMM_LEAD}
**Consumers:** Claude AI Agent
**Change control:** PR Review

---

Use this document to classify launches and set the right level of PMM work, research, and deliverables.

## Tier 1: Market-Expanding Alliances

**Scope:** Omni-channel campaign. High effort. Majority budget spend.

**Definition:** Revenue-driving launches that create direct business impact through major integrations, product launches, or large-scale collaborations. These launches need strong visibility and credibility.

**Marketing goals:**
- Increase conversions and user adoption
- Position the partnership as a key growth driver
- Maximize media coverage and community engagement

**Briefs:**
- Creative
- Content and website
- Slide deck

**Research:**
- Product analysis
- ICP, possibly net new
- Messaging and positioning framework

**Default deliverables:**
- X thread with key takeaways, relevance, @mentions, and internal amplification
- Newsletter mention with a short highlight
- Blog post with educational partnership content
- Press release distributed via PR agency
- Live audio/social discussion (e.g., X Space)
- Conference slide mention or logo for passive awareness-building

**Optional add-ons:**
- Joint webinars
- AMAs
- Additional co-marketing initiatives

**Use when:**
- Complexity is high
- Timeline is long
- PMM work needs quantitative research
- The launch is a major product launch, new brand moment, or other major announcement

## Tier 2: Brand Awareness and Ecosystem Growth

**Scope:** 3 to 5 channels, including social, content, and paid ads. Some budget spend.

**Definition:** Launches that increase brand awareness, educate users, and drive ecosystem growth without immediate revenue impact. These include thought leadership collaborations, technical integrations, or updates.

**Marketing goals:**
- Expand brand recognition in your industry
- Educate the audience on impact and value
- Strengthen industry positioning through strategic collaborations

**Briefs:**
- Content
- Website

**Research:**
- Messaging and positioning framework
- Use the previous ICP

**Default deliverables:**
- X thread with key takeaways, relevance, @mentions, and internal amplification
- Newsletter mention with a short highlight
- Blog post with educational partnership content
- Conference slide mention or logo for passive awareness-building

**Use when:**
- Timeline and complexity are moderate
- PMMs may need new supporting docs
- The work may require multiple briefs
- The launch is a significant feature, beta, or growth-oriented announcement

## Tier 3: Brand Visibility and Credibility

**Scope:** 1 to 2 channels. Mostly owned channels such as email and social. Low to no spend.

**Definition:** Low-impact releases that build brand visibility and credibility through ecosystem affiliations, small integrations, or community initiatives.

**Marketing goals:**
- Align with strong brands to reinforce credibility
- Maintain visibility in key industry spaces
- Build goodwill with minimal resource allocation

**Briefs:**
- Content

**Research:**
- Work from existing docs and assumptions

**Default deliverables:**
- X thread with key takeaways, relevance, @mentions, and internal amplification
- Newsletter mention with a short highlight
- Conference slide mention or logo for passive awareness-building

**Use when:**
- Turnaround time is short
- Complexity is low
- PMMs work from assumptions and existing docs
- The launch is a small feature announcement or low-lift visibility moment
