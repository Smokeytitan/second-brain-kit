# Marketing Brief Template

**Status:** Active v1.0
**Owner:** {PMM_LEAD}
**Consumers:** Claude AI Agent
**Change control:** PR Review

---

## Global Writing Rules

Use this template to create one concise marketing brief from PRD inputs.

### Non-negotiables
- Never use em dashes.
- Use active voice.
- Lead with the bottom line.
- Use plain language.
- Avoid jargon, buzzwords, and hype.
- Do not invent facts, metrics, dates, customers, or claims.
- Keep names, links, @mentions, and dates exactly as provided.
- Soften risky claims unless the source supports certainty.
- Use approved product names from your style guide, never legacy or internal names.

### Tone
- Professional
- Pragmatic
- Direct
- Slightly positive

### Format rules
- Follow the section order exactly.
- Stay within each section word limit.
- Keep each section short and specific.
- Use parallel structure in bullets.
- Make the customer value clear.
- If information is missing, write [Missing] instead of guessing.
- Only use spacing to divide sections

## 1. Brief Info
**Purpose:** Capture admin facts only.

**Word limit:** Each field 8 words max.

- **Owner:** [Name]
- **Launch Name:** [Describe the user outcome, not the mechanism. 6 words max.]
- **Product / Campaign:** [What this brief covers]
- **Business Unit:** [Team or org]
- **Tier / Priority:** [Tier]
- **Launch Date:** [Date]

**Fill-in rules**
- Keep each field to one line.
- Do not add strategy or messaging here.
- Write the launch name from the user's perspective. Name what they gain or what changes for them, not the feature or system enabling it.

## 2. Launch Summary
**Purpose:** Explain the launch fast.

**Word limit:** 50 words max.

**Fill in:**
Write 2 to 3 sentences that answer:
1. What will users or customers experience differently?
2. Who does this affect?
3. What is changing to make that possible?

## 3. Audience and Problem
**Purpose:** Define the target and the pain.

- **Primary Audience:** [5 words max]
- **Secondary Audience:** [Optional, 5 words max]
- **Core Problem:** [18 words max]
- **Why Now:** [18 words max]

**Fill-in rules**
- Name the audience clearly.
- State one core problem.
- Keep each line distinct.

## 4. Launch Scope and Value
**Purpose:** Explain what changed and why the customer should care.

- **Scope:** [20 words max]
- **Customer Value:** [18 words max]
- **Proof Point 1:** [10 words max]
- **Proof Point 2:** [10 words max]
- **Proof Point 3:** [Optional, 10 words max]

**Fill-in rules**
- Focus on this launch only.
- Write value in customer language.
- Use facts for proof points.

## 5. Messaging
**Purpose:** Lock the story.

- **Topline Message:** [15 words max]
- **Support Point 1:** [10 words max]
- **Support Point 2:** [10 words max]
- **Support Point 3:** [Optional, 10 words max]
- **CTA:** [8 words max]

**Fill-in rules**
- Write one clear message.
- Keep support points distinct.
- Do not restate proof points word for word.

## 6. Distribution
**Purpose:** Name the channels.

**Fill-in rules**
- Keep this high level.
- Name channels, not tasks.
- Put detailed execution in your project tracker.

## 7. Success
**Purpose:** Define the win.

- **Goal 1:** [10 words max]
- **Goal 2:** [Optional, 10 words max]
- **Goal 3:** [Optional, 10 words max]

**Fill-in rules**
- Use measurable outcomes.
- Limit to 1 to 3 goals.
- Pick goals tied to this launch.
